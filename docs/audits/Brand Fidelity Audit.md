# Brand Fidelity Audit

Verify that redesigns preserve brand identity and URLs.

---

# Rules

- Never change existing URLs without explicit decision.
- Never change brand identity without explicit decision.
- Document every change that affects brand or routing.
- Record the before and after state.

---

# What to Check

## URLs

- [ ] All existing URLs preserved.
- [ ] New URLs follow existing patterns.
- [ ] Redirects in place for any changed URLs.

## Brand Identity

- [ ] Logo and wordmark unchanged.
- [ ] Brand colors preserved (unless explicitly updated).
- [ ] Brand typography preserved (unless explicitly updated).
- [ ] Brand voice and tone consistent.

## Visual Identity

- [ ] Consistent visual language across pages.
- [ ] No jarring style changes between sections.
- [ ] Transition between old and new design is smooth.

---

# Documentation

Record all changes:

- What changed.
- Why it changed.
- Who approved the change.
- Impact on existing users.
